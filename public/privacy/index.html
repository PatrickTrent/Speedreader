Privacy

Kort in het Nederlands Trentelman AI Solutions in Groningen is verantwoordelijk voor Speedreader. Mail: speedreader@agentmail.to. Lezen gebeurt op jouw apparaat; wij krijgen je document niet. Vraag je een samenvatting, dan sturen we de tekst die je kiest — tot ongeveer de eerste 35.000 tekens — naar Google Gemini. Het bestand zelf uploaden we niet. Betalen gaat via Stripe. We bewaren geen documenten of samenvattingen op onze servers. Je kunt je privacyrechten uitoefenen via het e-mailadres hierboven, of een klacht indienen bij de Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Who is responsible

Trentelman AI Solutions, Groningen, the Netherlands, is the controller for Speedreader. You can reach us at speedreader@agentmail.to.

What we use personal data for

Reading. RSVP reading happens on your device. We do not receive the document or the text you paste, so we do not process it.

AI summary. If you ask for a summary, the text you choose, up to the first 35,000 characters, is sent to Google Gemini. The file itself is not uploaded. We do this to provide the summary you asked for. The legal basis is performing that service (article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Payments. If you buy credits, the payment is handled by Stripe, so we can carry out the purchase. The legal basis is the contract (article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Payment records are also kept because Dutch tax law requires it. The legal basis for that is a legal obligation (article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Fraud prevention. Stripe may use payment data to prevent fraud. The legal basis is legitimate interest (article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who receives data

Google, through the Gemini API, receives the text you submit for a summary. Stripe receives the payment when you buy credits. The page itself, including its styles, fonts, and the PDF and Word readers, is served by Speedreader. We do not sell personal data.

Transfers outside the EEA

Google and Stripe may process personal data in the United States. Where they do, that transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses. See Google’s privacy policy and Stripe’s privacy policy.

How long data is kept

We do not store documents or summaries on our servers. Credits stay in your browser until you clear them. Payment records are kept for 7 years (Dutch fiscal retention). For anything Google holds, Google’s Gemini API terms apply. For anything Stripe holds, Stripe’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of your data, and you can object to processing. Email speedreader@agentmail.to. You also have the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Cookies

Our site sets no cookies. If you buy credits, you pay on Stripe’s own checkout page (on stripe.com). That page applies Stripe’s cookie policy and Stripe’s privacy policy.

Decisions, accounts, and selling data

We do not make automated decisions that produce a legal effect on you, or a similar significant effect. There are no accounts. We do not sell your data.
