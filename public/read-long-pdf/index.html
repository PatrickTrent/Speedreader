FAQ

How do I skim a long PDF?

Open the reader, upload the PDF or paste a section, then choose Volledig Lezen. RSVP shows one word at a time. No account.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the reader, upload or paste, start RSVP.

What if I only need the key points?

Use AI Summary when you only need the key points. One credit is one summary. It covers roughly the first 35,000 characters (about the first 12 to 15 pages of dense text). RSVP the whole document, or paste a specific section for the summary. The text is sent to a cloud AI (Google Gemini). Do not upload secrets you would not paste into a cloud AI. Packs are Starter €0,99/5 and Pro €3,99/50.

Does a picture-only scan work?

The reader flashes words from the text in the file. A PDF that is only images has nothing to show. Paste the text, or upload a PDF you can select text from.
